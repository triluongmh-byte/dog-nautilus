# Deviations from the sealed contract (board domain)

Tester round 1 repairs are #3 and #4. Notes for integration:
1. Contract text: U1 "2x8 2.54 pins, long axis along X, USB-C at +X edge (60.75 centre)". The module (23 x 18 mm, centre x 49.25) extends to x 60.75, i.e. 0.75 mm beyond the 22 mm board at x = 60 and 0.25 mm beyond x = 38. Change: none (contract geometry kept; the footprint silk stays 0.5 mm inside the edge). Evidence: contract module size vs outline. Cross-team impact: case front wall/slot clearance must allow the 0.75 mm overhang (the contract already puts the USB centre at x 60.75).
2. Tooling note, not a contract change: `kicadpy.publish` writes its snapshot store to the parent of the project root, which is outside this sandbox. Publishing therefore runs from a copy: tools/board/publish.sh. Outputs in boards/ are the publisher's own.
3. Header plug height (carrier_component_zone 14 mm above the board top; OLED underside z 22.4). Contract text: every header with mating plug stays inside the zone. Finding: J1-J9 are straight 1xN headers (2.5 mm base + 6 mm pins); a common crimp housing (about 14 mm) would reach ~16.5 mm. Decision: the board keeps straight headers (changing to right-angle would put plugs across the 3.8 mm row pitch and invalidate the routed layout). The 14 mm zone is met only with LOW-PROFILE mating parts: plug body plus wire exit <= 11.5 mm above the header base (e.g. 2.54 mm IDC/crimp housings of <= 9 mm body, wires bent sideways immediately), or wires soldered/heat-shrunk straight to the pins and routed horizontally. Not verified with real plugs (assumption GIA DINH); bringup.md step 7 measures it; fallback is right-angle headers with a re-route (rows would need >= 6 mm pitch). Cross-team impact: case must keep the 1.8 mm slack between zone top (z 20.6) and the OLED underside (z 22.4) and route harness cables out sideways; the OLED/lid cannot close over fully upright 16.5 mm plugs.
4. U1 seating. Contract: module PCB bottom z 9.1 (2.5 mm above the carrier top), USB-C centre (60.75, 0, 11.9) aligned with the front slot. A 2.54 mm female socket would seat it 8.5 mm up (USB ~6 mm too high). Change: U1 is soldered on two 1x8 male headers whose plastic base gives exactly 2.5 mm (parts.json, assembly.md); the module is not pluggable but flashes through the front slot. No cross-team change versus the contract.
5. Labels: MH1-MH4 now follow (x,-y): MH1 KiCad (40.5,+23.5), MH2 (57.5,+23.5), MH3 (57.5,-23.5), MH4 (40.5,-23.5). C1 value is 1000uF 10V everywhere.
6. Servo supply wired, not via the carrier (2026-11-16). Board copper unchanged; J3-J6 pins 2-3 are left unplugged and servo red/brown go to a splice at BUCK1 (power.md, harness.md). Reason: 4 x 0.6 A stall exceeds the ~1.5 A of the 0.6 mm / 1 oz trunk.
7. BUCK1 changed from "MP1584-class" (contract example) to Pololu S13V30F5 buck-boost. Evidence: MP1584 modules test ~2 A continuous and need ~1.5 V headroom (>= 6.5 V in), but the pack falls to 6.0 V; S13V30F5 is specified 2.8-22 V in, ~3 A at 5 V. Cross-team impact: case buck_holder was sized for 22 x 17 mm; S13V30F5 size not measured.
8. C1 MPN changed from Rubycon 10ZLH1000MEFC8X11.5 (no such listing found; ZLH 1000 uF/10 V is 8x16 or 10x12.5) to Rubycon 10PX1000MEFC8X11.5 (LCSC C88853 listing, D8 x 11.5). Same footprint and value.
9. Harness fuse: an earlier plan said a 3 A PTC; chosen hold >= 3.5 A (4 A class) because PTC hold current derates with temperature and 2.65 A is the worst-case pack current.
10. Socket metadata: parts.json now records U1 seatHeightMm 2.5 (male headers, #4) so the published enclosure metadata stops assuming an 8.5 mm female socket.
11. CHG1 split into two modules (2026-11-16): no single USB-C 2S module with >= 5 A discharge was found. CHG1a USB-C boost charger with balance + CHG1b HX-2S-JH20 10 A protection board (parts.json offBoardModules, harness.md). Cross-team impact: CHG1b (46.7 x 23 x 3.2 mm) is taped on top of the cells, 6.5 mm below the lid; not checked in CAD.
12. BUCK1 S13V30F5 is 22.9 x 22.9 mm and does not fit buck_holder (22 x 17). Interim: skip buck_holder, foam-tape the module on the floor. Case source must be rebuilt with BUCK y1 = -9.7 where CadQuery exists.
13. Hand build on perfboard with through-hole R/C and 1N5817 instead of the 0805/SMA parts on the KiCad record (same values and nets; parts.json perfboardBuild). The KiCad board stays the schematic/ERC record and is not ordered.
